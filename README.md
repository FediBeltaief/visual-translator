## Version 3.0: Technical Overhaul & Awesome New Features!

This new version is a big step up! We've made lots of improvements under the hood to make the app faster and easier to use.

**What's New?**

1.  **Slick New Look & Feel**

We've totally redesigned the user interface (that's the part you see and interact with!). Now it's more interactive and works great no matter where you are in the world.

*   **Works on Your Phone!**: The app now looks and works perfectly on phones. Translate stuff on the go!
*   **Light or Dark Mode? You Choose!**:  Pick the light or dark theme that's easiest on your eyes. The app will remember your choice.
*   **Speaks Your Language**:  The app can now be used in English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, and Arabic.  Everything, from buttons to labels, changes instantly!

2.  **Smarter Text Reading (OCR) & Handwriting Recognition**

The way the app reads text from images is now much better. It can handle all sorts of tricky images.

*   **Better Image Processing**: The app now automatically improves images (makes them bigger, clearer, etc.) to better read handwriting and text that's hard to see.
*   **Reads in Paragraphs**: Instead of just seeing lines of text, the app now understands paragraphs. This helps it translate more accurately.

![alt text](https://i.imgur.com/lHrh9Gl.png)


3.  **Edit & Go!**

*   **Fix Mistakes Easily**: You can now correct any mistakes in the text the app finds. Just edit the text in the "Detected Text" box and click translate again.
*   **Faster Translations**: The app now uses a new system for translations, which means things happen much faster.
*   **Super Speed (If Possible)**: If your computer has a special type of graphics card (CUDA), the app will use it to read text even faster!
